Interval Route Finder

Loops that claim the most territory

    Finds the loop through each start point that encloses the most area without going over its length. Loops start and finish at the road junction nearest your pin. If a bigger loop is a short way off, the run goes out to it and back the same way. The search keeps going until it stops finding better loops; press Stop at any time to keep the best so far.

    Click the button, then click the map. Drag pins to move them. Each start point gets its own loop.
