--- E. HOW LOOPS ARE SCORED ------------------------------------------------

23. Start point mode maximizes enclosed AREA within the length limit. The
    alternative is maximizing roundness (isoperimetric quotient), as area
    mode does, which would favor shorter, rounder loops over bigger ones.
    Current assumption: maximize area.
    Answer: ok

24. Area mode: when two loops are about equally round and both meet the
    coverage target, should the shorter one win (less running), or doesn't
    it matter?
    Current assumption: roundness only.
    Answer: if the iso-quotient is equal, the one with the greater total area is the better one

25. Area mode: may the loop go outside the drawn shape? Right now roads up
    to 30% beyond the shape are allowed, and area outside the shape neither

    Answer: change to 20% outside

26. Default coverage target for area mode?

Done:
- Area mode: equal roundness -> the loop enclosing more area wins (Q24).
  Roads are loaded up to 20% beyond the area (Q25). The coverage target is
  now a slider with a plain-language explanation, default 90% (Q26).

  switch is gone, since it only changed the time estimate.
- Big searches (Q31-33). Measured limit for loading every road at once: a
  10 mi loop in central Chicago is ~80 sq mi, a 73 MB download (~50 s),
  ~200k intersections, ~650 MB of browser memory. So searches over 80 sq mi
  run in stages: a rough loop on major roads (downloaded in tiles), then
  every ~10 mi stretch is re-optimized with all roads within half a mile of
  it. Tested on a 15 mi start loop and an area loop. The loop updates live,
  Stop keeps the best so far, and it can be exported at any time. Search
  times now go up to 10 minutes.
- New "Place" tab (Q34): search a city, county, state, lake or park and its
  real boundary becomes the area.
- 3-point (rotatable) rectangle and freehand polygon (Q35). GPX export (Q36).
- "Find loops" skips start points whose loop is already up to date (Q37).
